What this does not say. Not that hedging makes a project greener, nor that a financial cost is an environmental one. Only this: a currency shock takes local cash that could have serviced the same asset, so the cost of avoided carbon rises with the exchange rate.

What this is not

Illustrative simulation, not advice. No probability is attached to any shock; the hedge price and its cost are typed by you, never quoted; debt amortises in equal slices, cash available is constant; no taxes, reserves, covenants or construction period. Comparing two scenarios shows a mechanism — not a proven effect.
