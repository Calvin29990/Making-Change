Méthode, hypothèses et références académiques

Cas pédagogique construit, non calibré sur une entreprise réelle. Les références encadrent la question de recherche, pas les paramètres numériques. DSCR inférieur à 1 : trésorerie insuffisante pour le service de la dette. Au-dessus de 1 mais sous le seuil choisi : dette couverte, marge cible non atteinte.

Couverture théorique au spot initial (655,957 XAF/EUR), sans courbe de taux ni contrainte de liquidité. CFADS constant : 850 M XAF/an. Frais appliqués au service annuel couvert. Résultats nominaux cumulés, non actualisés. Simulation, pas un conseil financier.

Assumptions and provenance

This is a constructed teaching case, not a published business-school case or an empirical company study. No real company is assigned invented financial data.

ParameterAssumption / status
LoanEUR 10 million · 15 years · 5% fixed interest: illustrative choices
CFADSXAF 850 million/year, constant: illustrative, not revenue
AmortisationEqual annual principal; interest on opening balance
FX stressImmediate, permanent parity break; no probability assigned
HedgeConstant share of each annual payment, locked at 655.957 XAF/EUR
Hedge chargeApplied to covered annual debt service converted at the locked rate
DSCR threshold1.20x starting assumption, not a documented lender covenant

The locked rate equals initial spot by construction. It is not an observed forward quote: interest differentials, tenor, liquidity, counterparty risk, collateral and hedge availability are not modelled. Even a positive charge does not make this a market-priced forward.

DSCR = annual cash flow available for debt service (CFADS) / annual debt service in XAF, including the illustrative hedge charge in the hedged scenario. CFADS does not react to FX, inflation or operations. The shock is applied in every year; results are nominal, not discounted.

Academic framework — not model validation

These selected references motivate the research question. None validates the numerical calibration, the flat hedge rate or a causal gain from hedging in this prototype.

  • Adler & Dumas (1984), Exposure to Currency Risk: economic exposure is broader than the contractual debt-service exposure isolated here.
  • Froot, Scharfstein & Stein (1993), Risk Management: financing frictions motivate hedging; investment and financing decisions are not endogenous in this model.
  • Kleimeier & Megginson (2000), Project Finance Loans: project-finance context, not a source for this loan's parameters.
  • Allayannis & Weston (2001): association in US non-financial firms, not causal evidence for an African infrastructure SPV.
