What the market side can and cannot do

These are daily reference rates, published by a third party, cached for twelve hours. They are not a dealer quote, not a bid/offer, and not an executable price. No forward points, no liquidity, no credit charge are included: the hedged rate and its cost stay user inputs.

Because most free providers quote against the euro, a pair is crossed through EUR when needed. A hard peg has no meaningful free-market series, which is exactly why the desk requires the shock to be labelled as a parity break.

Degradation is visible, never silent. If no provider answers, the page says assumption and the panel shows the scenario value with its date. A number whose provenance is hidden is worse than no number.
